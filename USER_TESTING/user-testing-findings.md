Usability Findings on Project-2

✅ What worked: I tested with my friends for 5 minutes. He seemed to understand that it was a website first off and doubted I created it. That’s off to a good start I guess. He seemed thrilled about the background image and the colors. He was also able to navigate with the back button across different pages. The fact he thought my work was AI sort of gave me a sense of happiness and comfort, because in this day and age AI use in academia is growing super rampant.


⚠️ What confused them: I noticed something that threw them off was the idea that they clicked on cards and nothing happened. If it were a real site that should not be an issue. I noticed the use of the website is not apparent. It is really a site about my favorite things.


🔄 What you changed: I added footer links to my social media site. This creates a sense of safety and security for the viewers of the website to leave remarks on my work. I am considering adding a foreword at the home page to make it more obvious that it’s a personal site about myself and my favorite things. 



🚫 What you didn’t change: I did not add links to the card because they’re not real products but I see that structure of the page does not immediately communicate what the intent of the site is for. The site is just about myself, and the things I like. A contact page was also provided. I also didn’t use the same background for all the pages. My decision is due to the short time provided, as I couldn’t come up with a solid UI design. But progressively, I will iterate changes over the pages and make their UI UX stronger.
